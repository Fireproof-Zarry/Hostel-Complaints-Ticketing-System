# User guide

## Signing in

Open HostelFix and choose your tab:

- **Student Login:** use your `@smail.iitm.ac.in` account. Other addresses won't work.
- **Staff Login:** for hostel staff and admins. Use the email address your admin added.

## Students

### Set up your profile

Open **My Profile** and add your name, phone number, roll number and room number. You need a room number to file a room complaint, and workers may call your number to arrange a visit.

### File a complaint

1. Open **File Complaint**.
2. Choose your room or a community area, such as a corridor or washroom. For a community area, enter the floor and the wing or area.
3. Pick a category, then enter a title and description.
4. Optionally add a photo, the times you're available, and whether your key is with security.
5. Submit it.

If a similar open complaint exists, you'll see a warning. If it's the same issue, upvote it instead of filing a new one. Otherwise choose **No, mine is different**.

You can have up to 5 open complaints at a time. Your draft is saved in that browser for 7 days.

### Follow your complaints

**My Complaints** lists everything you've filed. Choose **View** for the full details, the photo, the timeline, and the name and phone number of whoever is handling it.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting to be picked up |
| Assigned | Someone has taken responsibility for it |
| In Progress | Work has started |
| Resolved | It's been fixed |
| Rejected | The hostel office closed it without action |

You can delete a complaint while it's Pending.

To fix a mistake in the description, open the complaint with **View** and use **Edit complaint text**. You can edit it until the complaint is Resolved or Rejected.

### Nudge a slow complaint

If a complaint hasn't been resolved after a week, click **Nudge** on it in **My Complaints** to remind the admins.

- The button turns on at 5 AM on the 7th day after you filed it. Until then it's greyed out, and hovering over it shows how long is left.
- After a nudge, you can nudge again from 5 AM, 7 days later.
- Reload the page if the wait has passed but the button is still grey.
- You can't nudge a Resolved or Rejected complaint.

A nudge reminds the admins but doesn't change the complaint's status.

### Community issues

The **Community** page lists open issues in shared areas. Upvote the ones that affect you, so the hostel office can see what matters most.

### Notifications

Click the bell or open **Notifications**, then turn them on and allow them when your browser asks. You'll be told when a complaint's status changes.

Clicking a notification takes you to that complaint in **My Complaints**. Notifications stop on that device when you log out. On an iPhone, add HostelFix to your Home Screen first.

## Hostel staff

An admin adds your email first. Then use **Staff Login** with that account, and add your phone number under **My profile**, because students see it once you're assigned to their complaint.

### Your complaints

**My complaints** has two sections:

- **Assigned to me:** your active work, soonest deadline first. Overdue items are red.
- **Filed by me:** complaints you reported.

Click a row to open the complaint.

### Taking a complaint

In **Complaints**, find one that's unassigned and click **Assign**. Choose a priority and, if you like, a deadline. You can only assign yourself.

### Doing the work

Open the complaint and use the **Work Order Details** card:

- **Mark in progress** when you start.
- **Mark done** when it's fixed. You can add notes, and the student will see them on the timeline.
- **Cancel assignment** to return it to Pending.

### Changing a status directly

Click **Status** in the complaint table. Only allowed moves are offered, and you can reopen a Resolved or Rejected complaint.

### Filing a complaint

Open **File complaint** and choose a **Community area complaint** or **Room complaint**. For a room, enter its number, which is useful when a room is empty during vacations.

### Notifications

Turn on **Assignment notifications** at the top of **My complaints** to hear about new work. It only applies to that browser, and logging out stops it.

### Analytics

Open **Analytics** to see how the hostel is doing. Choose a date range and apply it, or leave it empty to include everything.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Headline counts and the monthly complaint trend |
| **Categories** | Complaints per category |
| **Floors** | Complaints per floor |
| **Recurring Issues** | The same kind of problem reported at least twice in one place |
| **Resolution** | Monthly filed and resolved counts, the resolution rate, and average resolution time |

Resolution rate is the share of complaints resolved, leaving out rejected ones. Admins see this page too.

## Admins

Admins can do everything staff can, plus the following.

### Nudges

Students can nudge a complaint that's been open for a week. Nudged complaints appear at the top of **Complaints** with a bell icon, latest first.

To be notified, click the bell in the top right of the page and allow notifications. Clicking a nudge notification takes you to that complaint and highlights it. The bell and the switch on **My complaints** control the same setting for that browser.

### Assigning complaints

Click **Assign** and choose anyone. Each person's current workload is shown beside their name. They'll be notified if they've turned notifications on.

### Managing staff

Open **Staff management**.

- **Add staff:** enter their exact email and click **Add staff**. "Hasn't signed in yet" shows until they first log in.
- **See workload:** each row shows active and overdue complaints. Click a row to see them.
- **Remove staff:** click **Remove**, then move their complaints to someone else or return them to Pending.

Admins themselves are set in the app's configuration, not on this page.

## Good to know

- Resolved and rejected complaints leave the admin list after 30 days, but students can still see theirs.
- Deleted complaints are removed permanently after 15 days.
- If someone else changes a complaint at the same moment, you'll be asked to refresh and try again.
